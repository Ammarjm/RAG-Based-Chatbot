Al-Balqa Applied University

Al-Balqa Applied University is a public university in Jordan. Its main campus is located in Al-Salt.

Faculty of Artificial Intelligence

The Faculty of Artificial Intelligence at Al-Balqa Applied University was established in 2019. It was established to provide academic programs related to modern technologies and artificial intelligence.

The faculty offers several programs in technological fields, including Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Data Science, Information and Cyber Security, and Virtual Reality.

Artificial Intelligence Major

Artificial Intelligence is one of the academic fields offered by the Faculty of Artificial Intelligence.

The Artificial Intelligence program focuses on developing students' knowledge and practical skills in intelligent computer systems.

Students study topics related to programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analysis, algorithms, and intelligent systems.

Machine learning allows computer systems to learn patterns from data and use these patterns to make predictions or decisions.

Artificial intelligence applications can be used in many areas such as healthcare, education, robotics, cybersecurity, business, transportation, and computer vision.

Students in the Artificial Intelligence field learn how to design and develop systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

The program combines theoretical knowledge with practical applications. Students can work on programming projects, machine learning models, data analysis, and intelligent applications.

Graduates can pursue careers in areas such as artificial intelligence development, machine learning, data science, software development, intelligent systems, and technology research.

The Faculty of Artificial Intelligence encourages students to develop technical, analytical, problem-solving, and programming skills.

Students can also participate in technology competitions, research projects, training programs, and practical activities related to artificial intelligence.
